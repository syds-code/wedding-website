Please join us on Saturday, March 4th, 2028, in celebrating our wedding at Catalonia Grand Costa Mujeres, an all-inclusive resort in Mexico!

Come enjoy a great all-inclusive resort experience where all of your meals and drinks are included with your stay while you celebrate our wedding with us.

To take care of your travel plans, we have been working closely with a travel agency that specializes in destination weddings, Vibe Thrive Travel. With their help, we have reserved a block of rooms with special group discounts at the resort.

We are currently holding most rooms from March 2nd–5th, 2028; however, you may book any dates you wish through the request form. Note, to take advantage of the special pricing, there is a 3-night minimum stay. If you can't stay for 3 nights, you're still welcome to book directly with the hotel or an outside travel service and join us for the celebration.

Transportation to and from the Cancún airport will be provided, but all flights should be purchased separately with your airline of choice.

Please reach out to Vibe Thrive Travel with any travel questions: info1@vibethrivetravel.com
