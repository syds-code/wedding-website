Resort Information

The Fives Beach Resort & Residences All Inclusive is just south of Cancún on the shores of the Riviera Maya. The Fives blends laid-back beach living with elevated design — spacious suites with full kitchens and private terraces, a string of pools leading down to the sand, and enough restaurants and bars that no two nights have to look the same.
